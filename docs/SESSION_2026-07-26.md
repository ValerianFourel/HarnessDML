## 1. INCIDENT — census fleet killed by a torch bump (fixed) · ADR 21

**Symptom.** Every `vllm serve` in the census/arms fleet crashed; census total
frozen at 1,110,391; all nodes hung to the 40-min health ceiling.
Cascade: `RuntimeError: operator torchvision::nms does not exist` → after
removing torchvision, `ImportError: vllm.vllm_flash_attn requires the CUDA flash
attention extensions (_vllm_fa2_C / _vllm_fa3_C)`.

**Root cause.** A stray `uv pip install` re-resolved deps and bumped
**torch 2.11.0 → 2.13.0+cu130** under the wheel-installed **vLLM 0.25.1**
(no `vendor/vllm` source tree — the wheel path won at setup, so vLLM cannot be
rebuilt locally). vLLM 0.25.1's compiled C-extensions (torchvision ops,
`_vllm_fa2_C/_fa3_C`, `vllm._C`) are ABI-linked to torch 2.11.0; a lone torch
bump silently breaks all of them. A bare `import vllm` still passed the whole
time (flash-attn loads lazily at `vllm serve`), which briefly masked it.

**Fix (confirmed live).** torch stayed CUDA-enabled → pure version skew:
```
pip install --no-deps --force-reinstall torch==2.11.0 torchvision==0.26.0 torchaudio==2.11.0
```
Verify the *serve path*, not just import:
`python -c "import vllm.vllm_flash_attn._vllm_fa2_C"`. Census resumed
1,110,391 → 1,118,301 within minutes.

## 6. CAUSAL-METHODOLOGY REVIEW — is the DAG's mediator treatment legit?

Two deep-research agents (opus) against the causal-mediation literature.

**Verdict (agent 1, fully cited).** Legitimate for 3 of 4 uses:
- **C1** "never condition on a post-treatment mediator for the TOTAL effect" —
  CORRECT (Rosenbaum 1984; Montgomery–Nyhan–Torres 2018). `load_panel` hard-
  error is the prescribed guardrail.
- **C2** "mediation via trace features" — **OVERSTATED**. Randomizing the
  components does NOT identify natural direct/indirect effects (the mediators
  aren't randomized); needs sequential ignorability (Imai–Keele–Yamamoto 2010);
  Robins–Greenland 1992 prove non-identification even in a randomized-exposure
  trial; a **treatment-induced mediator–outcome confounder** (latent per-rollout
  difficulty / sampled reasoning path) is near-certain and breaks NDE/NIE even
  with perfect trace measurement (VanderWeele–Vansteelandt–Robins 2014);
  multiple correlated mediators → recanting-witness non-identification
  (Avin–Shpitser–Pearl 2005). Point downstream users to **interventional**
  (in)direct effects, not natural.
- **C3** E[Y|answered] as a non-identified principal stratum — CORRECT
  (Frangakis–Rubin 2002).
- **C4** treatment→mediator as its own cost outcome — CORRECT (design-
  identified total effect on a downstream variable).

**Must-add to the paper (one sentence):** *only the components — not the trace
features — are randomized, so the design identifies total component effects but
not natural direct/indirect effects; mediation requires untestable assumptions
(sequential ignorability / no treatment-induced confounder) that plausibly fail
in agent rollouts, so we provision data for downstream mediation rather than
claim identified effects.* Also: the schema's pre-treatment "held-out
difficulty" cannot stand in for the *realized* per-rollout confounder.

**Verdict (agent 2 — measures-as-outcomes, fully cited).** Treating the trace
features as **outcomes** (not mediators) is the right call and HarnessLab's plan
is *largely best-practice*: cost as an unconditional total effect, a per-(model,
band) accuracy–cost **Pareto frontier** over the 32 configs, wall-clock
diagnostic-only, task-clustered SEs + sup-t bands, C_res conditional AND
unconditional, principal-stratum flag on E[Y|answered]. Five tightenings:
1. **Cross-outcome multiplicity is under-specified.** Declare whether the
   simultaneity family spans the outcome vector; if one omnibus table is wanted,
   build the sup-t band **jointly across outcomes** on resampled task clusters
   (so cross-outcome correlation enters the critical value). These are *multiple*
   endpoints (FWER: Holm / Romano–Wolf / FDR), not *co-primary* (FDA 2022).
2. **GPU-seconds is over-promoted.** It's hardware/serving-config-dependent
   (TP size, cache, node) → confounds capability with serving layout. Demote to
   a same-hardware efficiency outcome; make **characters** the cross-family cost
   unit (tokens are tokenizer-dependent, up to ~15× — Petrov et al. 2023,
   arXiv:2305.15425); tokens = within-model/billing only.
3. **"Cost per solved task" / "tokens per correct answer" must join the banned-
   headline list** — same post-treatment-conditioning error as E[Y|answered]
   (Rosenbaum 1984; Montgomery–Nyhan–Torres 2018). Report cost over ALL rollouts
   incl. timeouts/no_answer (where looping SR/R cost lives).
4. **Pareto-frontier membership is winner's-cursed** — extend the best-arm
   discipline (Hsu MCB + seed-split) from the single best cell to "config X is on
   the frontier" claims.
5. **Do NOT add SUR/MANOVA/desirability for estimation** — under the balanced
   design they give zero efficiency (Zellner aggregation) and destroy the
   per-outcome causal reading; keep any composite score descriptive only.
Anchors: Kapoor et al. "AI Agents That Matter" (2407.01502), HAL (2510.11977) —
both report accuracy–cost Pareto frontiers and find expensive agents rarely on
the frontier. New candidate refs (multiplicity, SUR, desirability, sup-t,
tokenizer fairness, HAL/Kapoor, SWE-bench/GAIA/AgentBench) listed for
`references.bib`. PDFs in `paper/literature/pdfs/`.
